# Effects and particles

Effects are presentation only. They never feed gameplay state, so these facts
pin parameters, timelines and geometry. Particle jitter is random in
presentation. The port does not reproduce the C generators' shared `MRrand`
draws, so no family records a draw sequence or an RNG state.

## Synthetic effects

`synthetic/effects.json` is generated by `tools/make_golden_effects.py`. This
independent Python interpretation reads frozen `PARTICLE.C`/`.H`
(generator initialisers 31–445, `Frog_pop_explosion_colours` 192–199,
`Explosion_offsets_octagon` 205–226, `Explosion_matrix` 272–276, the
callbacks 448–2724), `EFFECTS.C`/`.H` (`CreateShadow` 149, `UpdateEffects`
shadow 327–583 and trail 585–644, `RenderEffects` 822–1220,
`SetupABRChangeFT3` 1264, `CreateTrail`/`ResetTrail` 1307–1420, poly pieces
1915–2390), `FROG.C` (trail and shadow tables 130–179, `CreateFrog` 292–383,
hop triggers 1410–1446, `UpdateFrogEffects` 2444–2482, `FrogUpdateCroak`
2941–3042, `FrogKill` 3132–3206, `UpdateFrogBaseColour` 3309–3393,
`SetFrogScaleColours` 3420–3441, `FrogStartPolyPiecePop` 3643–3682),
`FROG.H` (jump times 52–54, `FROG_POLY_PIECE_POP_DURATION` 78, croak times
119–124, `FROG_POPPING_FIXED_OT` 141), `MISC.C`
(`GetWorldYQuadrantFromMatrix` 75–99), `ENTITY.H` (hilite types 51–61,
`WORLD_GRAVITY` 110), `ENTITY.C` (`Entity_special_particle_generators` 71–82,
`CreateLiveEntitySpecials` 1766–1878, `LiveEntityStartPolyPiecePop` 3406),
`COLLIDE.C` (`FrogSplashAnimList` 43–65), `scripter.c`
(`TurtleSplashAnimList` 31–55, `ENTSCR_CREATE_3D_SPRITE` 1411–1453), `score.C`/`.H`
(`Score_library` 26–51, sprites 107–226, lifetime 57), `ent_des.c`
(`Des_rock_shadow_offsets` 33–39, shadows 255, 1674–1729), `ent_org.c`
(gold glow 262–268) and `API.SRC/MR_SPRT.C`/`.H` (`MRCreate3DSprite` 253–333,
`MRProcessSpriteAnim` 975–999, opcodes 1006–1350). Arithmetic follows C:
arithmetic right shifts, division truncating toward zero, u32 and s16 wraps,
and 16.16 positions. Trigonometry uses the shared rounded 12-bit table and
`MRNormaliseVEC` the rounded float convention of `make_golden_paths.py`,
whose zero vector stays zero.

### Rules

`generator_parameters` lists all thirteen `PARTICLE.C` initialisers with
their PSX-source constants. Hilite exhaust, dust and fire, the frog slip,
water bubble and on-fire generators, the pop explosions, the pickup and gold
frog glows, both swarms and the stone-to-gold sparkle are included. Particle
polygons are FT4 with `setSemiTrans(1)`, except the swarm. Their blend rate
comes from the image's page word, the VLO `blend_mode`. Pop explosions use a
dummy FT3 with ABR 1. Displays cull a particle whose half width reaches 0x60.

`CreateLiveEntitySpecials` reads only static model 0 of an animated MOF. It
maps hilite types through `Entity_special_particle_generators`: types 3 and 4
use the exhaust and type 5 uses the dust generator. The `PARTICLE_CLOUD` enum
comment names the bison, but retail bison hilites are type 5. Type 6 uses
fire. Types 1–2 create a 3D sprite with a NULL animlist. Type 0 does
nothing, and types 7–8 reach a compiled-out assert.

The shadow phase is `FROG_JUMP_TIME − fr_count`. A super jump uses
`((15 − fr_count)·6)/15`. Either is clamped to 0–5. A stationary frog or an
on-spot jump uses phase 0. Phases 0–5 select offset tables 1, 2, 3, 4, 3 and
2 and textures `im_frog_shadow0`, 1, 2, 2, 2 and 1. The shadow is
hidden on an entity, while dying, at a checkpoint and while jumping without
`FROG_JUMP_TO_LAND`. Corners rotate the offsets by the frog's world-Y quadrant,
`m[2][2]` against `m[0][2]`, through `rcos`/`rsin(q·0x400)` and add the
translation.
`EFFECT_NO_ROTATION` (desert rock, hidden tumbleweed) adds the offsets
unrotated. If corners 0 and 1 are in different grid columns, it rotates the
corner order. That path sets `x2` from the stale `x0` and keeps that `x0`,
so the split test then never fires: an X-straddling rock shadow stays one
quad with four grid heights. The shown tumbleweed clears `NO_ROTATION` and
uses the quadrant.
Grid columns are `(v − Grid_base) >> 8`. A split happens when corner 0 and 2
differ in grid X or Z. Alignment is N when `v0.z − v2.z > 0x40`, else E when
`v0.x − v2.x > 0x40`, else S when `v0.z − v2.z < −0x40`, else W. The split edge is
`& ~0xff` of the named corners (N: `v0.z`/`v1.z`; E: `v0.x`/`v1.x`; S:
`v2.z`/`v3.z`; W: `v2.x`/`v3.x`). Split V is `te_v0 + (d·te_h)/y`, with
`y = offset0.z − offset2.z` and `d = v0.z & 0xff` (N), `v0.x & 0xff` (E),
`0x100 − (v0.z & 0xff)` (S) or `0x100 − (v0.x & 0xff)` (W). It is stored as a
byte. Split-edge heights interpolate the named map-poly vertices of the
square under corner 0 or 2 by the corner's own coordinate `& 0xff`, as the
`height` strings state. Other corners use `GetHeightFromWorldXYZ`. The
cases exclude heights. Shadows are FT4, rgb 0x303030, semi-transparent, at
`max(MR_OT_NEAR_CLIP, otz − 4)` when the averaged OT entry is inside the table.

A hop sets the trail colour to `(0x202020 + 0x606060) >> 1` = 0x404040, the
timer to 16 (26 for a super jump) and RESET. The next update rebuilds all six
sections from the current head pair, sets `current_section` 4 and clears
`display_sections`. Each update with a non-zero timer decrements it. The
colour falls by 0x080808 at or below 12 while non-zero, and otherwise rises by
0x080808 below 0x606060. The update writes the head pair at
`current_section`, moves it down with wrap and raises `display_sections` up
to 5. The update that reaches 0 still runs; the frog trail lacks
`EFFECT_KILL_WHEN_FINISHED`. Rendering needs a non-zero timer. The head
colour is the trail colour, and each older section is 0x101010 lower, floored
at 0. Head offsets are (∓0x40, −0x10, −0x20) in the frog frame. Trail
triangles are semi-transparent G3 in the global OT, followed by a dummy FT3
with ABR 1 at the last section's OT entry.

Poly pieces take a triangle origin as the C `/3` mean and a quad origin as
the `>> 2` mean. Start position is `(t + R·origin) << 16`. Velocity is the
normalised `R·origin − (0, 0x80, 0)` shifted left by 10. Each update with a
non-zero timer decrements it from 32. It adds the angular velocity to the s16
rotation and then (8, 0xc, 0x10) to the angular velocity. Each piece adds
`WORLD_GRAVITY << 1` (0x5b000) to Y velocity before moving. Render scale is
`min(0x1200, timer << 8)`. Pieces are unlit and drawn at OT 2. Frogs build
pieces only with more than `GAME_MAX_HIGH_POLY_PLAYERS` players. Single-player
frogs never pop into pieces; entity pops use the same rules.

3D sprites start with no image, scale 4<<16, rgb 0x808080, speed 1 and
count 1. Each `MRUpdateObjects` decrements the count. At zero it runs
commands until one stops and resets the count to the speed after each.
NOP, SETIMAGE and KILL stop. SETSPEED, SETSCALE and SETCOLOUR continue. KILL
keeps its PC and sets `MR_OBJ_DESTROY_BY_DISPLAY`. The splash shows ripple
images 1–6 from update 5 and fades through 0x606060, 0x404040 and 0x202020.
It is killed at update 23. Splash sprites lie in the XZ plane at OT offset
−0x10. Original-theme turtles create `formid − 18` ripples. In FrogLord's
form list, form 19 is `ORG_TURTLE`, 20 is `ORG_TURTLE2` and 21 is
`ORG_TURTLE3`. The ripples start at (0, −0x40, −(formid − 19)·128) and step
Z by 0x100. Form 18 creates none. Below 18 the unsigned count wraps
(`source_defined` false). Other themes create one ripple at the frame origin.

The frog pop ring is drawn while the generator life is non-zero. Its life
starts at 30 and falls by one per update. Scale is `((30 − life) + 10) << 1`.
The rotation is `(rcos, rsin)(Game_timer << 7)·scale >> 12`, stored as s16.
Each octagon point is that rotation applied with `>> 12`. Projection then
multiplies by `vp_perspective`/`SYSTEM_PERSPECTIVE` = 0x100/192. The cases
stop before projection. Outer vertices get (life·8, 0, 0). Inner vertices
get `life·Frog_pop_explosion_colours[player]`, with r in the low byte. The ring
uses ABR 1 at OT 2. The baby-frog headrush uses colour 4. On PC,
`Pickup_explosion_disp` is entirely `#ifdef PSX` and draws nothing.

Score sprites start at the given screen point with rgb 0x808080 and timer
45. Each update decrements the timer and is removed at zero, on update 45.
Otherwise it adds −0x8000 to the 16.16 Y. For
`45 − timer < 4` the grey is `(45 − timer)·0x20`; after that it is
`0x80·timer/45`.

A drowning frog's colour scale is `max(−(dy<<4) >> 9, 0)` for red. Swamp uses
`max(((dy<<5) >> 9) + 128, 0)` for green and `max((−(dy<<5) >> 9) + 32, 0)` for
blue. Other themes use `max((−(dy<<5) >> 9) + 64, 0)` and `+ 192`. Each value
keeps only its low byte; there is no upper clamp. The custom ambient is
(32, 64, 64). Here `dy = t.y − fr_old_y`. Outside cave-light maps the base
grey is `max(0, 0x80 − (x_overshoot + z_overshoot)/2)` against the fade
rectangle. Volcano DROWN and FLOP deaths call `SetFrogScaleColours(24, 0, 0)`.
They start `PGIN_frog_on_fire` with generator offset (0, −80, 40). A DROWN
death equate then has the water tint overwrite that scale every frame.

The croak needs a high-poly frog to animate. The press saves the light
radii and sets the rate to `max_r2/6`, both unsigned. Six inflate updates add
the rate to both radii and set scale `((6 − timer)·0xa00)/6 + 0x200`. Four
hold updates follow. Six deflate updates subtract the rate and set scale
`(timer·0xa00)/6 + 0x200`. The last restores the saved radii. Low-poly frogs
only save the radii and play the sound.

Glow pulse angles start at 0 and add 0x100 masked by 0xfff per move. The gold
glow uses particle 0 and the pickup uses particle 1; particle 0 indexes the
pickup image list. Magnitude is `(rsin(a) + 0x3800) << 3`. Half extents are
`((w·mag)/otz) >> 8`, or `>> 9` with more than two viewports. Glows draw
when `0 < otz < ot_size` and `|sy| < 0x300`. Pickup OT is `otz − 0x10` and
gold OT is `otz − 0x30`. The gold glow is created inactive and toggled by
the baby frog. Its display reads the frame translation, not the (0, −75, 30)
creation offset.

### PC transforms

The frozen engine applies these PC transforms, and the port follows them. The
trail and pop ring use ABR 1, which is additive. The tongue uses ABR 0
(`CreateTongue` passes 0 in retail). The hilite exhaust uses
`im_frog_smoke1`, texture 1163, from the retail record. Pickup and gold
glows use texture 1727, not `im_tongue_tip` 38. The gold glow colour is
(255, 255, 16), not (170, 170, 10). The `generator_parameters` cases keep
the C constants. The retail `pgen` rows below hold the executable's values,
and the port reads those.

### Exclusions

The families exclude particle jitter, every `rand()` draw and the shared RNG
state. They also exclude render-rate state: the on-fire `pt_user −= 4` and
dust `pt_user += 1` per render, the pickup image step on `MRFrame_index`
0, and `pp_frame_index`. Projected screen positions and terrain heights are
excluded, as are the swarm's camera-facing quads.

### Reproduction and deliberate breaks

Run `python3 -B tools/make_golden_effects.py` to verify reproducibility, or
add `--write` to regenerate deliberately. These deliberate-break paths, one per
family, must each fail `test_effects`:

- `generator_parameters[3].expected.particle_max_life`
- `frog_shadow_phase[20].expected.count`
- `frog_shadow[5].expected.corners[2][1]`
- `shadow_split[0].expected.polys[1][0].z`
- `hop_trail[1].expected.rows[13].rgb[0]`
- `poly_piece_pop[0].expected.rows[10].positions[1][1]`
- `splash_timeline[0].expected.rows[16].rgb[0]`
- `splash_offsets[4].expected.sprites[2][2]`
- `pop_ring[1].expected.rows[5].inner_rgb[2]`
- `score_popup[0].expected.rows[10].rgb[0]`

Change one, require the focused test to fail, then restore it and rerun.

## Retail effect resources

`pc-retail-v3.0e/effect_resources.tsv` comes from
`python3 -B tools/make_golden_effect_resources.py --data-dir <game-folder>`.
`FROGGER_DATA_DIR` is the default folder. The generator verifies by default
and rewrites with `--write`. It reuses `make_golden_mof.py`'s archive and
model readers, `make_golden_exe_tables.py`'s executable and texture-pointer
readers, and FrogLord's PC image name list (`imageList` of the version
configuration). Every row kind has its own `#` header line, and each row
begins with its kind.

- `hilite`: `resource_id`, `file_name`, `static_index`, `part`,
  `hilite_index`, `type`, `type_name`, `attach`, `index` and `special`. The
  file holds each row, as `models.md` describes. `special` is the
  executable's generator for static model 0, `sprite`, `none`, `assert` or
  `unused` for later static models.
- `hilite_borrowed`: incomplete parts whose declared hilites come from
  `counterpart`.
- `hilite_census`: `type`, `type_name`, `count` and `files` for types 0–8.
- `hilite_generator`: `Entity_special_particle_generators` read from the
  executable.
- `pgen`: every `MR_PGEN_INIT`-shaped record (2D type, flags 0 or
  `MR_PF_NO_GEOMETRY`, callback pointers). Records are named through the
  executable's hilite and `Frog_particle_effects` tables. Other records are
  named in C declaration order by flags, maximum particles, generator life
  and `user_data_1`. `texture_ids` resolves `user_data_2`. `c_differences`
  lists fields that differ from `generator_parameters`.
- `frog_particle`: `Frog_particle_effects` flags and single/multiplayer
  generators.
- `image`: `symbol`, `texture_id` (its `bmp_pointers` index), `resolution`,
  `width`, `height`, `flags` (distinct values across banks), `blend_mode`,
  `banks` and `exe_refs`. `exe_refs` counts aligned words addressing the
  texture record outside `bmp_pointers`. `resolution` is `pointer:<index>`,
  then `exe:<records>` when executable data names it, `fixed:pc_glow` for
  texture 1727, and `name:<name>` when FrogLord's PC list names the id. A
  FrogLord name must equal the symbol without `im_`. An unknown symbol is an
  `unresolved` row.
- `score_library`: the executable's `Score_library`, checked against
  `score.C`.
- `pop_colour`: `Frog_pop_explosion_colours` read after the hilite fire
  record. The generator checks them, the octagon offsets, the shadow offset
  tables and both splash animlists against the C.

The retail executable disagrees with the frozen C in three places.
`PGIN_hilite_exhaust` has scale `74 << 9` (0x9400) and texture 1163;
the C has `64 << 9` and `im_tongue_tip`. `PGIN_hilite_fire` has lifetimes
30–40 and scale `75 << 10`; the C has 10–20 and `70 << 10`. A third
explosion-shaped record at 0x475E80 (`user_data_1` 0xC00) shares
`Frog_pop_explosion_disp` and has no C name. The ripple images are 1726,
1722, 1723, 1724, 1725 and 1721. FrogLord's list comments them out, so
their only source is the executable's animlists. The port reads these
records from the supplied executable and follows them, as it does every other
table; the C constants are only the fallback when a build's records cannot
be located, and the retail test pins the `c_differences` column.

`test_effects` covers every synthetic family except `poly_piece_pop`, which
`test_effects_pieces` checks through `PolyPiecePopState`, and `splash_offsets`,
`gold_glow` and `pickup_glow`, whose tests land with the entity effect views
(P11c).

`test_retail_effects` compares the port's decoded hilites, generator
records, effect texture ids, score library and pop colours with these rows.
For the retail deliberate break, change the `DES_BISON.XAR` part 7 `hilite`
`type` (5), the `im_ripple_tim1` `image` `texture_id` (1726) and the
`PGIN_hilite_fire` `pgen` `particle_min_life` (30). Require
`test_retail_effects` to fail, then restore all three.
